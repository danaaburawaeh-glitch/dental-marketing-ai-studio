# Dental Marketing AI Studio v1.4.0 — Marketing OS

First public distribution release of Dental Marketing AI Studio.

This release expands Assistant Studio from a primarily Instagram-growth system into a broader marketing operating system for dentists and dental clinics.

- Marketing Offer Architect
- Paid Media Planner
- Lead Funnel Optimizer
- Lead Follow-up Manager
- Campaign Director
- Marketing ROI Analyst
- Local SEO / GEO Strategist

### Operating workflow

Strategy → Offer → Campaign → Paid Media → Funnel → Lead Follow-up → ROI

Instagram, content planning, WhatsApp lead response, system orchestration, knowledge management and existing growth-analysis capabilities remain included.
